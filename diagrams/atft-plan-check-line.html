The plan-check line

atft/.assembly-lines/careful-plan-check.dot — the planner's next task is extracted and reviewed on its own before the doer starts. Setting PlanCheck:Enabled in appsettings.json to false skips the whole review. The three validators (testability, UX, OO design) are drawn as one node, and the start node is omitted.
